What I borrowed from Patagonia

  • Andes on the horizon: snow-capped peaks, far away at the Creek and filling the sky by the lakes.
  • Steppe: golden coirón grass tufts and ochre cliffs (bardas) along the rivers.
  • Lupines: the purple and pink spikes along the creeks.
  • Poplar rows in the river valleys, and lenga forest around the mountain lake.
  • Cobble beaches instead of sand, and clear blue to turquoise glacial water.
